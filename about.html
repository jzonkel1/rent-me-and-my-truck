How it started

From Pizza Money to a Real Business

On February 9th, 2023, Eddie was looking at a truck he drove barely 100 miles a month and a new trailer he'd used a handful of times. He thought about all the jobs he'd already done for friends — and how getting paid in pizza wasn't quite cutting it.

He was up until 3 a.m. that night doing research. By sunrise he had a name and a plan. The rest, as he puts it, is history.

Since then, Rent Me and My Truck has grown on one simple thing: showing up, doing the work right, and treating people well — whether it's a single couch or a whole property, a proud homeowner or someone whose place just got away from them. We get our hands dirty so you can keep yours clean.
